# Business Logic

## Categories
Finance Operations, Vendor Management, Budget Control, Contract Administration, Compliance Follow-up, Reporting, General Operations.

## Confidence Thresholds
- 0.90-1.00: strong recommendation
- 0.75-0.89: good recommendation
- 0.60-0.74: needs human review
- below 0.60: request more information

## Human Review Required
Payment, contract, audit, compliance, legal, and low-confidence recommendations require human review.
